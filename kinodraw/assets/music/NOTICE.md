# Bundled music

All three tracks come from FreePD.com, a public-domain music library. FreePD's catalogue page states:
"100% Free Music - Free for Commercial Use, Free Of Royalties, Free Of Attribution, Creative Commons 0.
It is 'copyright free' to the extent that the law allows."

FreePD.com has since closed. The files were retrieved unmodified from the Internet Archive's
captures of their original URLs (https://freepd.com/music/<Title>.mp3), captured together with
https://freepd.com/upbeat.php (2024 snapshot). Checksums are in `tracks.json`.

| File | Title | Composer |
|---|---|---|
| fresh_focus.mp3 | Fresh Focus | Kevin MacLeod |
| natural_vibes.mp3 | Natural Vibes | Kevin MacLeod |
| inventing_flight.mp3 | Inventing Flight | Bryan Teoh |

Attribution is not required. Crediting the composers in video descriptions is still appreciated.

platform flags a video, dispute it citing CC0 and this notice.
