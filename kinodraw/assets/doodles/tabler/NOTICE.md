# Tabler Icons: source and licence

Imported from Tabler Icons (https://tabler.io/icons), outline style; line icons drawn with round ink lines.
Source: https://registry.npmjs.org/@tabler/icons/-/icons-3.49.0.tgz. Licence: MIT, see `LICENSE` (the pack's own file).
`MANIFEST.json` lists the source file and licence of every picture.

## What was modified

Regenerate with `python -m kinodraw.library.packs tabler SRC_DIR` (kinodraw/library/packs.py):
- Files renamed `tb_<snake_case_name>.svg`; cleaned by the library's SVG sanitiser
  (inline geometry only); transforms baked into absolute path data; art re-fitted to a 320x320 box
  with 16 px padding; shapes converted to paths (arcs stay arcs), coordinates rounded to 0.1 px.
- Every paint (including currentColor) set to the library ink #1B1B1B; lines redrawn 11 px wide with round caps and joins.
- Tags (`../tags/tabler.json`): `desc` from the icon name, `en` from its name and the pack's
  own keywords, `category` from the pack. No Chinese keywords: these pictures are found in English
  and Spanish only.
