KinoDraw

Privacy policy

Last updated October 4, 2026. This covers the KinoDraw app (called Doodle Studio before version 0.2.0), its KinoDraw Cloud AI director and the optional voice server. The Chrome extension for teachers has its own policy.

KinoDraw turns a script you write into a hand-drawn video on your own computer. The app and KinoDraw Cloud have no ads, no analytics and no tracking; the site counts visits without cookies (see below), and nothing about you is ever sold.

The offline director: nothing leaves your computer

With the offline director you need no account. Unless you choose your own voice server (below), your scripts, projects, narration and videos stay on your computer. The app only downloads its voice model (from GitHub; the Chinese voice also needs a small settings file from Hugging Face) and its doodle-search model (from Hugging Face) the first time they are needed. Those downloads send nothing about you beyond what any download does: GitHub and Hugging Face see your IP address. (Before version 0.2.0, if Hugging Face could not be reached, the Chinese doodle-search model came from Google Cloud Storage instead.)

KinoDraw Cloud, the AI director (optional)

If you sign in with your email and choose "KinoDraw Cloud AI", this is what is sent, and to whom:

KinoDraw Cloud stores your email, your plan, the install ID, counts of your videos and their AI cost, and a scrambled (hashed) form of your sign-in token, never the token itself. It never stores your script, the suggestions, your narration or your video. A Gmail address counts as one account however it is written (with dots or a "+tag").

Sign-in codes are deleted when used, or within about two days after they expire. Rate-limit records, which contain an IP address or email, are deleted within about four days. On your computer, the sign-in token is kept in your system's keychain.

Your own AI key or program (Advanced, optional)

If you turn on Settings > Advanced directors and choose "My OpenAI key", "My Anthropic key" or "OpenAI-compatible server", the app sends the same section request (sentences, titles, language, picked phrases and candidate doodle names) straight from your computer to that provider with your key: OpenAI, Anthropic, or the server address you entered. KinoDraw Cloud is not involved, and that provider's own terms and privacy policy apply. With "My own command", the app hands the request to the program you chose on your computer; where it goes from there is up to that program. Your keys and command are kept in your system's keychain, never in project files.

Your own voice server (optional)

This is off by default: the built-in voice reads your script on this computer. If you turn on Settings > Voice server, the app sends each beat's text (the words to be spoken), the model and voice names, and your API key if you entered one directly to the server address you choose, and nothing else. KinoDraw does not run that server or see those requests; the server's own terms and privacy policy apply. Your key is stored in your system's keychain for that address, never in project files, and is sent only to the address it was saved for. Only your own settings choose the server: a project file someone sends you cannot. Test (whether it is on or off) and Hear it (while it is on) send it one sample sentence. To offer the server's voices as choices, the app also asks that address for its list of voice names when you press Test and, while it is on, each time Settings, New video or a project's Narrator tab opens. That request sends only the model name (and your key for that address, if you entered one); for OpenAI's own API, which has no such list, it shows OpenAI's documented voices without asking. Your own recordings stay on your computer.

This website

This site counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless (it sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device), and it is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose server logs record your IP address when you visit. Its Download button asks GitHub's API for the latest release so it can offer the file for your computer.

Deleting your account

To delete your KinoDraw Cloud account and its usage counts, email privacy@doodlecloud.org from the address you signed in with.

Children

KinoDraw Cloud accounts are not meant for children under 13. Anyone can use the offline director, which needs no account and sends nothing unless you choose your own voice server.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page and its date change with it. Questions: the project's GitHub page or privacy@doodlecloud.org.
