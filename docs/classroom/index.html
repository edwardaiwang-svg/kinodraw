KinoDraw for Classroom

Turn your lesson script into a hand-drawn video, read aloud, and add it to your Google Classroom class. It is all made right in Chrome: there is nothing else to install.

Add to Chrome · coming soon

A Chrome extension for teachers. It works with the Google account you teach with, in Chrome on Windows, Mac and Chromebooks.

A 224-word science script about the water cycle, turned into this 3-minute video in Chrome.

Three steps

1 · Open your class

Open a class you teach in Google Classroom.

2 · Make a doodle video

Click the Make a doodle video button at the bottom right of the class page.

3 · Give it your script

Paste it, or choose a Word or text file. The video goes to your class, or waits as a draft for you to check.

Made for young learners

  • Every word written on the board is said as it is written, so reading and listening go together.
  • A simple cartoon picture is drawn for what is said, as it is said, and a list is drawn in full.
  • Each part ends with its key idea on a sticky note, read aloud. Captions and gentle music are included.
  • Headings in your script (or Word's Heading styles) become the video's parts.

Private by design

  • Only teachers see the button. Students simply watch the video in Classwork.
  • The video is made on your computer and saved to your own Google Drive.
  • KinoDraw never reads your students' names, work or grades. Your script's sentences are sent only to suggest pictures, and are not kept.
