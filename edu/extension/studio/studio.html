Turn your lesson into a hand-drawn video

Write or upload a short script. KinoDraw draws it on a whiteboard, reads it aloud, and puts the video in your Google Classroom class.

Use the Google account you teach with. KinoDraw asks to see which classes you teach, to save the video to your Google Drive, and to add it to your class's Classwork. It never sees your students' work or grades.

KinoDraw for Classroom is for teachers

This Google account doesn't teach any active Google Classroom classes.

If you teach with a different account, sign out and sign in with that one. If your school's Google admin controls apps, they may need to allow KinoDraw for teachers.
