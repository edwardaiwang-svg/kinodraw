KinoDraw for Classroom

You're all set!

KinoDraw turns your lesson script into a hand-drawn video, read aloud, and adds it to your Google Classroom class. It all happens right here in Chrome: there is nothing else to install.

  1. Open a class you teach in Google Classroom.
  2. Click Make a doodle video at the bottom right of the class page.
  3. Give it your script: paste it, or choose a Word or text file. KinoDraw draws it, reads it aloud and posts it to your class, or saves it as a draft for you to check first.
Open Google Classroom

Only teachers see the button: students just watch the video in Classwork. Your first video downloads the voice and drawing kit (a few hundred MB, once), so give it a few extra minutes. Tip: click the puzzle piece in Chrome's toolbar and pin KinoDraw; its icon opens the studio from any page.
